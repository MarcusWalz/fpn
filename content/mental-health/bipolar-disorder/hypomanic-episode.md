## DSM-V Criteria for Hypomanic Episode
**Must have all 6 criteria**
- A distinct period of abnormally and persistently elevated, expanisve, or irritable mood AND abnormally and persistently increased activity or energy, **lasting 4+ consecutive days, present most of the day, nearly every day**
- During the period of mood/energy disturbance
