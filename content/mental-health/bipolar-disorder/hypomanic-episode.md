## DSM-V Criteria for Hypomanic Episode
**Must have all 6 criteria**
- A distinct period of abnormally and persistently elevated, expanisve, or irritable mood AND abnormally and persistently increased activity or energy, **lasting 4+ consecutive days, present most of the day, nearly every day**
- During the period of mood/energy disturbance must have **3+ symptoms** (4 if mood is only irritable) and must be different from typical behavior
  - Inflated self-esteem/grandiosity
  - Decreased need for sleep
  - Pressured speech
  - Flight of ideas/subjective racing thoughts
  - Distractability
  - increase in goal-oriented activity/psychomotor agitation
  - Excessive involvement in activities w/ high potential for painful consequences
- This must be an unequivocal change in functioning that is uncharacteristic when not symptomatic
- It must be observable by others
- It is NOT severe enough to cause marketed impairment or necessitate hospitalization. There are no psychotic features
- It is not attributable to the effects of a substance or other medical condition

Note: Common in Bipolar I but not necessary for diagnosis
