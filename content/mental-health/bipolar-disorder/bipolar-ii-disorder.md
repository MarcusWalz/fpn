## Defintion
- [Hypomanic episode](hypomanic-episode.md) + Major depression
- No history of mania 

## Possible DSM-V Specifiers w/ Bipolar II
- **Anxious Distress**: Associated w/ higher suicide risk, longer duration of illness, and greater likelihood of non response. Requires 2+:
  - Feeling keyed up
  - Feeling on edge
  - Difficulty concentrating due to worry
  - Fear that something aweful might happen
  - Fear of losing control 
- **Atypical Features**: Possible when depressed. Sleeping/eating more, feeling heavy or leaden
- **Catatonic**: During depression ONLY, otherwise is Bipolar I
- **Melanchonia**: possible when depressed
- **Mixed Feature State**: Symptoms of depressed mood AND hypomania in same episode
- **Psychotic Features**: Yes, only when depressed, otherwise Bipolar I, specify as mood(episode) congruent or incongruent
- **Rapid Cycling**: NO. Only Bipolar I.
- **Seasonal component**: Hypomania is often triggered in spirng or with change in light or shift work
