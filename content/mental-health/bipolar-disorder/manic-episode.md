## DSM-V Criteria for a Manic Episode
**Must meet all 4 criteria**
- A distinct period of abnormally and persistently elevated, expansive, or irritable mood AND abnormally and persistently increased activity or energy. Must last for **1+ week and be present most of the day, nearly every day** or require hospitalization
- **During mood/energy disturbance 3+ of following symptoms must be present** to a significant degree and represent a noticable change in behavior
  - Inflated self-esteem or grandiosity
  - Decreased need for sleep
  - Pressured speech
  - Flight of ideas/experience that thoughts are racing
  - Distractability
  - Increase in goal-directed activity, or psychomotor agitation
  - Excessive involveent in activiites that have a high potential for painful consequences
- The mood disturbance must be severe enough to cause **marked impairment in functioning or necessitate hopsitalization** to prevent harm to self or others **or psychotic features are present**
- The episode is not atributable to the physiologic effects of a substance or other medical condition

Note: If a manic episode emerges during antidepressent treatment and then persists past termination of the treatment it meets full criteria
