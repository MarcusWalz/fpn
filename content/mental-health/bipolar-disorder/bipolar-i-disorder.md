## Definition
- Manic or mixed feature episode +/- psychosis +/- major depression

## DSM-V Diagnostic Criteria for Bipolar I
**Must meet all 4 criteria for at least 1 lifetime [manic episode](manic-episode.md)**
- May be followed by [hypomanic](hypomanic-episode.md) or major depressive disorder episodes

## Possible DSM-V Specifiers w/ Bipolar I
- **Anxious Distress**: Associated w/ higher suicide risk, longer duration of illness, and greater likelihood of non response. Requires 2+:
  - Feeling keyed up
  - Feeling on edge
  - Difficulty concentrating due to worry
  - Fear that something aweful might happen
  - Fear of losing control 
- **Atypical Features**: Possible when depressed. Sleeping/eating more, feeling heavy or leaden
- **Catatonic**: possible in both Mania and Depression
- **Melanchonia**: possible when depressed
- **Mixed Feature State**: Symptoms of depressed mood AND mania/hypomania in same episode
- **Psychotic Features**: specify as mood(episode) congruent or incongruent
- **Rapid Cycling**: 4+ mood episodes in last 12 months w/ partial or full remission of at least 2 months or a switch to an episode of opposite polarity
- **Seasonal component**: Hypomania is often triggered in spirng or with change in light or shift work
