## Why It Matters [@marzani.neff_2021]
- Affect more than 1% of the world's population
- Relapse rate is more than 70% over 5 years
- 75% of pts with bipolar disorder have a history of three additional/concurrent mental health conditions
-33% will attempt [suicide](../suicide/suicide.md) and 6-7% die by suicide

## AAFP Key Recommendations for Practice [@marzani.neff_2021]
- Screen all pt >12yr for depression
- Acute Mania: 
  - Monotherapy: Lithium, valproic acid, and some antipsychotics
  - Combo therapies: Lithium or valproic acid w/ quetiapine or risperidone
- Acute Bipolar depression
  - Monotherapy: Quetiapine and cariprazine
  - Combo therapies: Lurasidone w/ litium or valproic acid
- Maintenance : Lithium or Quetiapine or combo quetiapine + lithium or valproic acid

## Clinical Presentation
- Typically present for treatment w/ depression or anxiety. Physicians should explicitly ask about symptoms of mania/[hypomania](hypomanic-episode.md). 

## Evaluation
- Screen all pt >12yrs for depression using Patient Health Questionnaire. 
- Mood Disorder Questionairre can exclude Bipolar but is not sufficient for diagnosis
