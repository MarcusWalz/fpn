## Clinical Features
### Presentation
- Disclosure of SI
- After attempt
### Signs and Symptoms
- Psychological pain
- Impulsivity
- Help-seeking behavior

## Screening
- Screen for SI in pts w/ current psychiatric diorder or Hx of suicide attempt by asking directly about SI and suicidal behavior
- Columbia-Suicide Severity Raiting Scale: An option but labor intensive and not clear if this adds value
### Classify intentional self-injurious thoughts and behaviors
#### Nonsuicidal thoughts/behaviors
- **Suicide Gesture** : Action intended to lead others to think that one wants to die
- **Suicide Threat** : Thought/Statement intended to lead others to think that one wants to die
- **Nonsuicidal self-injury** : Intentional self-injury, not for others, w/o intent to die
- **Nonsuicidal self-injurious thoughts**: Thoughts of intentional self-injury, not for others, w/o intent to die

#### Suicidal thoughts/behaviors (including passive)
- **Suicide Attempt** : Self-harm w/ intent to die
- **Preparatory Suicidal Act** : Suicidal behavior that does not result in self-harm but is done with intent to die
- **Suicide Plan** : Development of specific plan to take action w/ intent to die
- **Active Suicidal Ideation** : Expressed desired to die w/o specific plan
- **Passive Suicidal Ideation** : Expressed wish or hope that death will occur without specific plan or desire to take action towards death
