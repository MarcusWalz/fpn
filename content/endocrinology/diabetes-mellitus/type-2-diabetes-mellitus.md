## Overview
### Prevention
#### Lifestyle Behavior Change
- Refer all T2DM to diabetes prevention program. Goal: Weight reduction of 5-7% + >150 min/week of physical activity
- Recommend Mediterranean-style, plant-based, DASH, or low-carb diets to all T2DM
#### Pharmacologic Interventions
- Metformin
	- *Most useful ppl <59 yrs; Hx of GDM; BMI >= 35; A1c >6; Fasting glucose >110.* 
	- Very useful for people on long term glucocorticoids
	- Check B12 levels annually as metformin can cause low levels
	- When paired w/ lifestyle modification = 50% reduction in DM risk
- SGLTi and GLPs +/- GIP
  - Metabolic benefits + weight loss
