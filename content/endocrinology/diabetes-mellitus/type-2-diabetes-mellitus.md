## Overview
### Prevention
#### Lifestyle Behavior Change
- Refer all T2DM to diabetes prevention program. Goal: Weight reduction of 5-7% + >150 min/week of physical activity
- Recommend Mediterranean-style, plant-based, DASH, or low-carb diets to all T2DM
#### Pharmacologic Interventions
- Metformin
	- *Most useful ppl <59 yrs; Hx of [GDM](gestational-diabetes.md); BMI >= 35; A1c >6; Fasting glucose >110.* 
	- Very useful for people on long term glucocorticoids
	- Check B12 levels annually as metformin can cause low levels
	- When paired w/ lifestyle modification = 50% reduction in DM risk
- SGLTi and GLPs +/- GIP
  - Metabolic benefits + weight loss 

### Comprehensive Medical Evaluation and Assessment of Comorbidities
#### Bone Health
- DEXA for all >= 65 and >=50 if use insulin or DM for 10+ yrs. Repeat q3yr.
- FRAX is NOT useful in this population as signficantly underestimates
- General Recommendations
  - Ensure adequate intake of protein
  - Ca2+ (1000-1200 mg)
  - D (serum levels 20-30 mg/mL; greater if taking bisphosphonates)
  - Stop smoking
  - Regular physical activity
- Pharmacological treatment: Consider if *T-score <-2, FRAX >= 3% for hip or 20% for major fracture*
	-  **Denosumab** - shown in phase 3 FREEDOM study to be efficacious. MUST be able to maintain the every 6 mo injection or may experience rebound bone loss. 
	- **Bisphosophonates** - may be more appropriate if gaps are expected 
	- **Teriperatide** - Osteoanabolic. Only for very high risk
#### Cancer
- Increased risk of cancers of liver, pancreas, endometrium, colon, rectum, breast, and bladder
- Undergo age appropriate screenings
#### Cognitive Impairment/Dementia
- Significantly increased risk and rate of cognitive decline
#### Dental Care
- Periodontal disease is more severe w/ higher A1c 
- Discuss oral health with all diabetics
- May need to adjust therapy during procedures
#### Disability
- Assess for disability and functional decline annually (ADLs and IADLs)
- Assess for  [Frailty](../../geriatric-medicine/frailty.md) annually
- Behavioral Health -  Anxiety, Depression, [Diabetes Distress Scale](https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/10523/diabetes-distress-scale-dds17$0) (DDS) 
#### Sexual Health in Men
- Screen for erectile dysfunction (52.5%). Treat w/ PDE5i
- Hypogonadism is common
#### Sexual Health in Women
-  Female sexual dysfunction is very common - consider screening with the [Female Sexual Function Index (FSFI)](https://www.apta.org/contentassets/a10dbafd45234c7a98ae3dd98385cdab/fsfi_test.pdf$0). Score ≤ 26 is (+) 
- Treatment -> Lifestyle factors, improve glycemic management, hormonal therapies, consider FDA-approved meds for hypoactive sexual desire disorder
#### Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease ([MASLD](../../gastroenterology/liver/metabolic-associated-steatotic-liver-disease.md)) and [Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatohepatitis](../../gastroenterology/liver/metabolic-dysfunction-associated-steatohepatitis.md) (MASH)
- *Screen all adults with T2DM for MASH spectrum*  using FIB-4 score even if they have normal liver enzymes
  - FIB-4: estimates risk of hepatic cirrhosis. Age, AST, ALT, platelets. 
    - <1.3 is low risk
    - ≥1.3 is higher risk of clinically significant fibrosis;  Get a Liver Stiffness Measurement. <8.0kPa has good NPV. Continue to follow in Primary Care w/ repeat testing q3yrs. 
    - ≥2.67 high risk of advanced fibrosis ->  refer to GI w/o additional testing
- “The goal of screening for MASLD is to identify people with at-risk MASH to prevent future cirrhosis, HCC, liver transplantation, and all-cause mortality (193–196).” pg S76
- ~70% of people w/T2DM have MASLD
- ~50% of T2DM + MASLD have MASH
##### Management
- Weight loss: 5-10% loss improves liver histology. Metabolic surgery can improve MASH and cardiometabolic health.
- Semaglutide is FDA approved for treatment of MASH.
- Pioglitazone has also been shown to reverse steatohepatitis. 
- Use statins unless decompensated cirrhosis. 
#### Obstructive Sleep Apnea
- Refer for sleep study if indicated
