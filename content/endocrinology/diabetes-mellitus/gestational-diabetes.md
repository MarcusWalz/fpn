## Overview
### Screening
**Table 2.8—Screening for and diagnosis of GDM** [@ADA_2026]

**One-step strategy**
- 24-28 weeks gestation; 75-g fasting OGTT, measurement at 0,1,2 hrs
- Test (+) if ≥ following values:

| Time | Plasma glucose |
|---|---|
| Fasting | 92 mg/dL (5.1 mmol/L) |
| 1 h | 180 mg/dL (10.0 mmol/L) |
| 2 h | 153 mg/dL (8.5 mmol/L) |

**Two-step strategy**

*Step 1:*
- 24-28 weeks gestation; 50-g *nonfasting* OGTT, measurement @ 1hr.
- Test (+) if 1hr PG ≥130. Then proceed to 100-g OGTT

*Step 2:*
- 100-g **fasting** OGTT
- Test (+) when 2+/4 ≥ following values (Carpenter-Coustan criteria):

| Time | Plasma glucose |
|---|---|
| Fasting | 95 mg/dL (5.3 mmol/L) |
| 1 h | 180 mg/dL (10.0 mmol/L) |
| 2 h | 155 mg/dL (8.6 mmol/L) |
| 3 h | 140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L) |

Notes: GDM, gestational diabetes mellitus; GLT, glucose load test; OGTT, oral glucose tolerance test.
