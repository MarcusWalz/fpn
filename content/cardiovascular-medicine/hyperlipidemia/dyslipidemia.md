## Overview
Use [PREVENT](https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/10491/predicting-risk-cardiovascular-disease-events-prevent$0) equations for adults 30-79 years. Reclassify if needed with Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) scoring
- LDL Lowering therapy should be considered for adults with 10 yr PREVENT-ASCVD risk estimate from 3-10% (borderline and intermediate)
	- Also for all adults aged 40-75 w/ [diabetes](../../endocrinology/diabetes-mellitus/diabetes-mellitus.md), CKD 3/4, HIV. All adults over age 75
- ApoB testing can help guide therapy and improve risk assessment
- Lp(a) should be measured at least once to identify individuls at higher risk of as ASCVD. Enhances risk if >= 125 nmol/L (1.4x increased ASCVD risk)

#### Screening in Children and Adults
1. Adults - Screen @ age 19 and every 5 years to identify ASCVD risk.
2. Children aged 9-11 - 1 screening to identify significant lipid disorders such as familial hypercholesterolemia. 
3.  Children >= age 2 - 1 screening if 1st or 2nd degree relatives with significant lipid/CV issues.
