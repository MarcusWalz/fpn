## Overview


## Screening
- Adults: Screen @ age 19 and every 5 years to identify ASCVD risk.
  - Use [PREVENT](https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/10491/predicting-risk-cardiovascular-disease-events-prevent$0) equations to assess ASCVD 10 and 30 year risk for adults 30-79 years. Reclassify if needed with Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) scoring
  - LDL Lowering therapy should be considered for:
    - adults with 10-yr PREVENT-ASCVD risk estimate >3%
    - Also for all adults aged 40-75 w/ [diabetes](../../endocrinology/diabetes-mellitus/diabetes-mellitus.md), CKD 3/4, HIV. 
    - All adults over age 75
- Children aged 9-11: screen once to identify significant lipid disorders such as familial hypercholesterolemia. 
-  Children ≥ age 2: screen once if 1st or 2nd degree relatives with significant lipid/CV issues.
### Recommendations for measurement 
#### Lipid Panel (TC, LDL, HDL, TG, and Non-HDL)
- Start w/ non-fasting unless TG ≥400 then get fasting to assess for hypertrigliceridemia
- Only use Lipid panel to screen children unless significant genetic risk
#### ApoB
1. In adults on LLT w/ ASCVD, Metabolic syndrome, T2DM, or elevated TG *measure apoB* to guide decisions for more intensive treatment. 
2. In adults not on LLT, measure apoB for better risk assessment. 

**Rationale:** ApoB directly quantifies the number of atherogenic lipoproteins which is more accurate than LDL which reflects the cholesterol mass carried by LDL particles. *This is cliniclally relevant in populations with metabolic syndrome* who may have atypically low LDL readings even with high Apo B. Possible nonstatin therapeutics include:
- Ezetimibe
- Bempoedoic Acid
- PCKS9 monoclonal antibodies
- Inclisiran
#### Lp(a)
1. In all adults, measure Lp(a) once for ASCVD risk assessment (nonfasting).

**Rationale:** “An *Lp(a) concentration of 125 nmol/L* (50 mg/dL) is considered high and is *associated with approximately a 40% relative risk increase in ASCVD* (Table 4) compared with individuals with low Lp(a) levels.” [@blumenthal.etal_2026] “As *Lp(a) concentrations remain similar over time and are minimally modified by lifestyle factors*,14 a single measurement is generally sufficient. Secondary causes of high Lp(a) include kidney, liver, or thyroid disease; pregnancy; menopause; and some medications.” [@blumenthal.etal_2026]

#### Monitoring and Follow-Up
1. For pts on LLT, clinicians should perform a lipid profile *4-12 weeks after initiation or dose change* and *every 6-12 mo after* to assess efficacy and adherence. 
	- Nonfasting unless high TGs then fasting

#### Prevention of Dyslipidemia
1. All components of AHA's Life's Essential 8 are imperative to optimize CV health w/ a RRR of 50% of adverse CVD outcomes even in individuals with genetic predispositions.
	- Healthy Diet, Regular Brisk Physical Activity, Maintaining a Normal Weight, Tobacco Cessation, Healthy Sleep, Stress Management, Control Cholesterol, Control Blood Sugar

-----
#### Dietary Management of LDL Disorders
1. Diet should emphasize intake of *fruits, veg, nuts, legumes, whole grains, and fiber*.
	- Mediterranean, DASH, and Vegetarian diets
	- Avoid saturated fats (red meat, butter, highfat milk, coconut oil, palm oils)
	- *Only a modest reduction likely (<25 mmol/L)*
#### Dietary management of Hyper-TGs
1. In adults w/ *fasting TG 150-499 mg/dL*, a diet low in sugar, refined carbs, saturated fats and alcohol may reduce TG.
2. In adults w/ *fasting TG 500-999 mg/dL*, a diet low in sugar, refined carbs, saturated fat and *no alcohol*, with limited fat, may reduce TG
3. In adults w/ *fasting TG >= 1000*, a diet that is very low in total fat and refine carbs with *no alcohol or added sugars*, may reduce TG
4. Additional beneficial lifestyle factors include: 5-10% weight loss, moderate-to-vigorous physical activity (>= 150 mins), full body resistance exercise 2days/week.

**Rationale**: 
- Lifestyle management *may result in a more than 70% reduction in TG* in highly responsive individuals. 
- Nutrition and weight managment a are the foundation. Obesity increases CV risk  through worsening of CKM (metabolic) syndrome risk fasctors, inflammation, and adiposopathy. 
	- **Ketogenic diets** often lower weight and TG in short term BUT may raise LDL substantially. 
	- Counsel an RDN if possible
- Physical activity often results in ↑ HDL, ↓ LDL and TG. Use a reliable physical activity assessment tool to promote. 
- *No dietary supplements* have been shown to significantly decrease LDL when compared with placebo.

#### Primary Prevention in Adults
##### General Principles
- In adults aged 30 -79 use PREVENT-ASCVD Equations for 10 year estimated ASCVD risk. It corrects the overestimated risk assessed using previous equations. 
	- Low <3%
	- Borderline 3-5% 
	- Intermediate 5-10%
	- High >= 10%
- Consider *risk enhancers*
- *CAC scoring* may help to decide whether or not to initiate therapy with clinicial/pt uncertainty
###### Risk Enhancers
(missing image: Pasted image 20261028101151.png)

- *Consider testing high sensitivity CRP if risk is borderline.* If there is no identifiable underlying cause consider high intensity statin therapy. 
- Women have special considerations w/ *early menarche (<10 yrs), PMOS, early menopause <40 yrs and adverse pregnancy outcomes* acting as risk enhancers
###### Selective Imaging - CAC scoring
- If decision is uncertain use CAC scoring. Below is for Borderline and Intermediate PREVENT-ASCVD Risk scoring
- **If CAC score is 0** + preference to avoid pharmacology + no high risk conditions -> *defer therapy and reassess w/ CAC in 3-7 years.* CAC = 0 is strongly associated with a low 10-yr risk of ASCVD. In *adults with DM*, is low for 5 years, then reassess. *Smokers* remain at higher risk.
- If CAC >0, especially if >100 (75%) highly recommended to initiate pharmacotherapy
- **==Once LLT is initiated CAC is not indicated b/c often incrases even though may stabilize or reduce plaque==**
- In persons w/ likely non calcifed plaque (DM, Inflammatory disorders, HIV) use Coronary CTA to inform risk.

##### Adults 30-76 yrs w/ LDL 70-189 mg/dL w/o ASCVD
- Low (<3%) Risk
	- *LDL<160 md/dL*  - Counselling on Health Behaviors
	- *LDL 160-189* - Moderate intensity statin is reasonable to reduce cumulative exposure to atherogenic lipoproteins
- Borderline (3-5%) and Intermediate (5-10%)
	- *Borderline*: Moderate intensity statin (30-50% LDL reduction). *==Goal LDL <100 and non-HDL < 130==*
	- *Intermediate*: Moderate intensity statin (30-50% LDL reduction) or High intensity statin (>50% LDL reduction). *==Goal LDL <100 and non-HDL < 130==*
- High (>=10%)
	- High intensity statin (>= 50% LDL reduction) recommended
	- *==Goal: LDL <70 and non-HDL <100==*
		- *Add Ezetimibe* if needed
		- Then *add PCSK9 mAB*  or *bempedoic acid* if needed
- Discontinue if life expectancy <1 yr
- If LDL 160-190 and family history of CAD or severe hypercholesterolemia (LDL >190,. non HDL >220, ApoB >140) - Refer to Genetics
##### Adults w/ LDL >190 mg/dL (severe hypercholesterolemia)
- First ==rule out secondary causes==
	- Dietary Ketosis, Hypothyroidism, CKD, Diabetes, Obesity, High dose thiazide diuretics, Glucocorticoids, Estrogens, Androgens, Menopausal transition, Pregnancy (more these are most common)
- Use specific risk scores NOT PREVENT-ASCVD
	- FH-Risk Score is a good option
- Initiate Maximum tolerated statin  in all individuals no matter CAC score
	- If *no clinical ASCVD* and *no additional risk*  ==Goal: LDL <100, nonHDL <130==
	- If *additional risk* but *no clinical ASCVD* ==Goal: LDL<70, non HDL <100==
	- If *Clinical ASCVD* ==Goal: LDL <55; non HDL <85==; ApoB <55
- If *unable to reach goals* Add ==Ezetimibe==, then ==PCSK9 mAb==, then ==bempedoic acid==. If *still unable to reach goal* switch ==Inclisiran as alternative to PCSK9 mAb==
- Refer to Genetics
##### Adults w/ DM w/o Established ASCVD
- In all adults 40-75, *initiate moderate intensity statin* w/ ==Goal: LDL< 100, non HDL <130== , TG <150 (add ezetimibe, PCSK9 mAB, bempedoic acid as needed)
	- If statin = side effects then just use ==Ezetimibe== +/- ==bempedoic acid== +/- ==PCSK9 mAb==
	- If LDL @ goal *on statin* ==but TG>150==, add ==Icosapent Ethyl== (specialized Omega 3s)
-  If high risk or w/ multiple risk factors *initate high intesnity ==statin==* w/ Goal: ==LDL <70 and non HDL <100==
- In adults >75 yrs and estimated life expectancy >2.5 yrs consider moderate intensity statin
- ==In adults 20-39== yrs w/ ==Diabetes Specific Risk Factors== {>=10 yrs T2DM or >=20 yrs T1DM, or albuminuria (>30 albumin/cr) or eGFR <60, or retinopathy/neuropathy} ==initiate moderate intensity statin==

#### Secondary Prevention 

##### Clinical ACVSD
###### Not @ Very High Risk
- *Initiate high intensity statin*. Goal: ==LDL <70, non HDL <100== ==Add Ezetimibe, PCSK9 mAB and/or bempedoic acid as needed.==
- Consider lowering Goal to LDL <55 and non HDL <85
######  Very High Risk
- 2+ major ASCVD events OR 1 major + >=2 high risk conditions (Age >65, coronary bypass, smoker, DM, CHF, HTN, LDL >100 on statins)
- *Initiate high intensity statin*. ==Goal: LDL <55 and non HDL <85==. ==Add Ezetimibe, PCSK9 mAB and/or bempedoic acid as needed.== It is reasonable to add *Inclisiran* as needed. 
###### HF w/ rEF due to ASCVD
- Reasonable to *start moderate intensity statin* 
- Do not start if no ASCVD risks.
##### Subclinical Coronary Atherosclerosis (Men >40, Women >45)
- If CAC >= 1000, treat w/ high intensity statin. Goal: LDL <55 and non HDL<85
- If CAC 100-999, treat w/ high intensity statin. Goal: LDL <70, non HDL <100
	- If 300-999 consider lowering goal.
- If CAC 1-99, treat w/ moderate intensity statin. Goal LDL<100 and non HDL <130
- If Incidental CAC on nongated CT start moderate or high intensity statin w/ Goal LDL 70 and non-HDL <100
##### CKD 3+
- If 40-75 and LDL 70-189 initiate ==moderate intensity statin +/- ezetimibe==
- If Clinical ASCVD initiate ==high intensity statin +/- ezetimibe +/- PCSK9 mAB.== ==Goal: LDL <55, non HDL <85==
- If on dialysis is reasonable to continue statin use

#### Pregnancy and Lactation
- People w/ hypercholesterolemia who are not high risk should *stop statin 1-2 mo* *before attempting to become pregnant or when pregnancy is discovered. 
- If Severe fasting hypertriglyceridemia (TG>500) use fibrates (post T1) and high dose omega3 ethyl esters 
- If ^ LDL but normal TG, use bile acid sequestrants
- May be reasonable to continue statins if genetic issue or clinical ASCVD - use pravastatin (hydrophilic)

### Hypertrigliceridemia (>150)
(missing image: Pasted image 20261028115939.png)

- If ^ TG + ASCVD = maximize statin +/- Icosapent Ethyl
- If ^ TG w/o ASCVD or DM = use PREVENT-ASCVD
- If TG >500, Estimate PREVENT-ASCVD, maximize statin +/- fibrinic acid or prescription omega 3 FA to lower TGs
- If TG >1000, Refer to Lipid specialist

### ^ Lp(a) (>125 nmol/L or 50 mg/dL)
- Maximize statin +/- PCSK9 mAb to achieve goal. 

### Side Effects
#### Statin Attributed Muscle Symptoms
- Evaluate for secondary cuases
- Use objective measures of clinical strength + CK to assess severity
- Reduce statin then discontinue and switch to other therapies
- *Do not use CoQ10 due to no benefit*
#### Medication Safety and Therapy-Associated Side Effects
- DM - continue statin therapy
- Stable liver disease - continue statin therapy
- *No need to monitor LFTs if no symptoms of hepatotoxicity*
- Many known drug interactions. Use app to assess.
